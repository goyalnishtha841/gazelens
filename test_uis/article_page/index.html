Why quiet mornings are making a comeback
By Alex Chen · 6 min read
Hero image
Across a dozen interviews, one habit kept coming up: people are deliberately leaving their mornings unscheduled. Not as a productivity hack, but as a quiet rebellion against a calendar that fills itself.

The pattern shows up in what researchers call “slack time” — unstructured minutes that, paradoxically, make the rest of the day easier to plan.
